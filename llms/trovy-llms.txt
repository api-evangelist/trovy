# Trovy

> Trovy (Trovy Technologies, Inc.) is a Kleiner Perkins-backed fintech building "the homeowner platform" -- consumer credit products that turn home equity into a flexible financial tool. Its flagship Trovy HELOC Card is a credit card powered by home equity, alongside the Trovy 1Loan home-equity loan. Trovy is a consumer product and publishes no public developer API, docs, or SDK surface as of July 2026.

Trovy takes its name from trouver -- French for "find" -- and the treasure trove. Founders: TJ Milani (CEO) and Ashley Harris (COO). Products: Trovy HELOC Card and Trovy 1Loan, with rewards, balance transfer, referral program, and a payment calculator.
